## Theme 1: Informative missingness in clinical time series

- **Lipton, Kale & Wetzel (2016)** — "Modeling Missing Data in Clinical
  Time Series with RNNs" (arXiv:1606.04130). Showed binary missingness
  indicators improve prediction over imputation alone. Basis for our
  Representation B.
- **Che et al. (2018)** — "Recurrent Neural Networks for Multivariate
  Time Series with Missing Values" (GRU-D, Scientific Reports). Named
  "informative missingness"; added time-decay on missingness. Basis for
  our Representation C.
